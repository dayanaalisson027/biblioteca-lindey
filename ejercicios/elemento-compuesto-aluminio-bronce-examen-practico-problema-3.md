Un cilindro de aluminio y su núcleo de bronce son sometidos a una carga axial P = 9 kip. Determinar el esfuerzo normal de cada uno de ellos, si E aluminio = 10×10³ ksi y E bronce = 15×10³ ksi.

Datos de la figura: radio exterior de 2 in, radio del núcleo de 1 in y altura de 1.5 ft.

[en]
An aluminum cylinder and its bronze core are subjected to an axial load P = 9 kip. Determine the normal stress in each of them, if E aluminum = 10×10³ ksi and E bronze = 15×10³ ksi.

Figure data: outer radius of 2 in, core radius of 1 in and height of 1.5 ft.
