La viga ABCDEF tiene voladizos AB y EF, apoyo articulado en B y móvil en E; carga P en A, Q en F, momento M₀ horario en C y M₀ antihorario en D. Por el método de la viga conjugada, con E = 200 GPa y sección b = 40 cm × h = 95 cm: P = 150 + W/10 kN, Q = 100 + W/10 kN, M₀ = 80 + W/10 kN·m; LAB = 1.5 + X/100, LBC = 3.5 + Y/100, LCD = 5 + Z/100, LDE = 3.7 + Y/100, LEF = 2 + X/100 (m).

a) Deflexiones por tramo, en A y F, y deflexión máxima (mm).

b) Giros por tramo y su variación.

* W, X, Y, Z = últimos 4 dígitos del código (N0000WXYZ).

[en]
Beam ABCDEF has overhangs AB and EF, a pin support at B and a roller at E; load P at A, Q at F, a clockwise moment M₀ at C and a counterclockwise moment M₀ at D. Using the conjugate beam method, with E = 200 GPa and section b = 40 cm × h = 95 cm: P = 150 + W/10 kN, Q = 100 + W/10 kN, M₀ = 80 + W/10 kN·m; LAB = 1.5 + X/100, LBC = 3.5 + Y/100, LCD = 5 + Z/100, LDE = 3.7 + Y/100, LEF = 2 + X/100 (m).

a) Deflections by segment, at A and F, and maximum deflection (mm).

b) Slopes by segment and their variation.

* W, X, Y, Z = last 4 digits of the student code (N0000WXYZ).
